## Summary

Built a 2x4 grid of archetype cards showing 8 canonical systems-thinking archetypes (Limits to Growth, Tragedy of the Commons, Fixes That Fail, Shifting the Burden, Eroding Goals, Escalation, Success to the Successful, Growth and Underinvestment). Each card shows a mini R/B loop schematic and name. Click a card to populate the detail panel with: a two-loop schematic, generic template description, public-health example, and a leverage-point note.

A "Search by behavior" mode selector lets students start from a behavior pattern (exponential growth, oscillation, drift, plateau) and jump to the matching archetype.
